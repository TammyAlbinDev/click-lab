# Beach Quiz

![My wireframe](wireframe.png)

## What it is
A quiz game about beaches and oceans.

## Who would use it
Anyone who wants to test their knowledge about beaches and oceans.

## What happens when you click
- Click an answer → The quiz tells you if you are correct or wrong.
- Click Hint → A hint appears.
- Click a correct answer → Your score increases.
- Click Start Over → The quiz resets so you can play again.
